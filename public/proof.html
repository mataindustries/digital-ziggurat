Major projects

PermitPulse

A live parcel research service for Los Angeles, and the case workspace behind it. Each brief checks official zoning, permit and fire hazard sources, then labels every finding Supported, Conflict or Unknown, with its citation.

  • Every finding cited
  • Unknown stays unknown
  • Reviewed by a person

Goal. Turn inconsistent public records into cited parcel research with visible uncertainty.

My decisions. I shape the research workflow and evidence boundaries: findings stay Supported, Conflict or Unknown, and delivery requires human review.

AI contributions. AI helps organize case material, implement the workspace and run quality checks. It does not make permit decisions.

Verification. Findings are tied to official sources and reviewed by a person; packet outputs retain citations, conflicts and gaps.

Working result. A live research offer and case workspace with evidence records, timelines and reviewed HTML/PDF packets.

Useful for. Custom research tools, operational interfaces and reports people can inspect.

DaemonV12

A headless music engine built for coding agents. There is no model inside: the agent writes every note as data through nine MCP tools, and DaemonV12 renders it offline to WAV, MP3 and stems, with effects and automation. GLASS//FIRE, a 24-second piece an agent composed entirely through MCP, is the production proof.

  • 9 MCP tools
  • Deterministic WAV + stems
  • v0.7 sampler articulation

Goal. Give coding agents instruments they can control through structured data and a validated tool interface.

My decisions. My tool design keeps composition as data, rendering deterministic and the language model outside the engine.

AI contributions. Agents compose through nine MCP tools. The engine validates their inputs and renders the audio.

Verification. The documented proof includes pinned GLASS//FIRE audio tests, SHA-256 render provenance and a clean-room package test in CI.

Working result. The v0.7 public beta provides an instrument and rendering tool for coding agents. GLASS//FIRE is the earlier v0.6 proof.

Useful for. Creative tools, MCP integrations and reproducible media workflows.

Dead Channel

An investigator enters an abandoned aerospace plant with a vibe-coded spirit detector. Its readout glitches during the approach, then returns to zero. It has been broadcasting all along. The swarm stays unseen, carried by 240 synthesized sound sources and the air of the building.

  • 240 virtual sound sources
  • 1280 × 720 · 60 fps

Goal. Make an invisible threat feel present through sound, environmental effects and timing.

My decisions. I created and directed the film and approved the picture, credits, captions and preservation of the original stereo mix.

AI contributions. AI-assisted visual production; original Python/NumPy sound under my creative direction using Claude-assisted production, in a separate pipeline from DaemonV12.

Verification. The case study connects measured stereo changes with verified picture frames; release checks preserve the approved V4.2 master identity.

Working result. An approved 22-second V4.2 horror-comedy with an accessible screening page and measured Signal Trace.

Useful for. Cinematic web experiences, technical creative production and accessible media presentation.

How I build with AI

  1. Find the real problem. Start with the workflow, constraint or failure that is actually costing someone time.
  2. Specify the behavior. Define what should happen, what must not change and how success will be tested.
  3. Use the right model for the job. Give coding agents narrow, testable work instead of asking one model to own the whole product.
  4. Verify before it ships. Run the tests, inspect the output and keep the evidence.
